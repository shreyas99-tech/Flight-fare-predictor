🧠 Scalable Nationwide Airline Intelligence

01

30 Cities & Tier Dynamics

Covers Tier 1 metros, Tier 2 commercial hubs, and Tier 3 tourist/regional gateways. The model understands that low-frequency regional routes carry different pricing dynamics than busy metro corridors.

02

Multi-Factor Realism

Factors in spatial distance (km), weekend travel surges, festive/monsoon seasonality, and flexible ticket options alongside advance booking days and duration.

03

HistGradientBoosting Regressor

Trained on 12,000 flight records using HistGradientBoostingRegressor with OneHotEncoder(sparse_output=False), providing fast, non-linear regression with over 96% accuracy.

04

Instant Live Valuation

Runs seamlessly on Flask (Port 5001) with responsive JavaScript and animated INR currency counters, allowing side-by-side execution with other applications.

Built strictly with Python, pandas, scikit-learn, joblib, Flask, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
